FAQ

Is Cakely free? Yes. Every feature is free, with no ads and no account.

I'm not getting reminders. Check that notifications are allowed for Cakely in iPhone Settings → Notifications, and that reminders are on in Cakely → Settings. You can choose the time and how many days before you're reminded.

Import from Contacts found nobody. Only contacts with a birthday saved in the Contacts app are listed. Make sure Cakely has access in iPhone Settings → Cakely → Contacts.

What about February 29? In years without a leap day, Cakely celebrates on February 28.

Can I move my birthdays to a new phone? There is no account sync. Use Settings → Export birthdays (CSV) to keep a copy.
