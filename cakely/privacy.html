Cakely — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary: your birthdays stay on your device

Cakely has no accounts, no server and no ads. The names, birthdays, notes and gift ideas you add are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to us or any third party.

Contacts

If you choose Import from Contacts, Cakely asks for permission and reads the names and birthdays of your contacts on your device so you can pick who to add. Contacts are never uploaded or shared, and Cakely never writes to your address book. You can turn this permission off at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications

Birthday reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is used.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information is collected by us (name, email, location, contacts)
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No account or registration

Purchases

The current version of Cakely has no in-app purchases. If purchases are offered in the future, they will be processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete a person in the app to remove their birthday, reminders and gift ideas. Deleting the app removes all data.
