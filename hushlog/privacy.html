Hushlog — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary: your baby's data stays on your device

Hushlog has no accounts, no server, no ads and no AI. Your baby's name, birth date, feeds, sleep, diapers and notes are stored only in a database on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to any third party, and they are never used to train AI models.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information (name, email, location, contacts)
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No ads and no data sharing with ad networks
  • No account or registration

Purchases

Hushlog Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

Feeding and trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is used.

Health information

Hushlog is a logging tool and does not provide medical advice. Data you enter is not shared with Apple Health or any other service. If you export a CSV file, you decide where it goes.

Deleting your data

Delete a baby in Settings to remove all of its entries, or delete the app to remove everything.

Children

Hushlog is used by parents and caregivers. It is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

سياسة الخصوصية

لا يتطلب Hushlog أي حساب، ولا يملك خادمًا، ولا يعرض إعلانات ولا يستخدم الذكاء الاصطناعي. تُحفظ بيانات طفلك (الاسم وتاريخ الميلاد والرضعات والنوم والحفاضات والملاحظات) على جهاز iPhone فقط ولا تُرسل إلينا أو إلى أي طرف ثالث. تتم الاشتراكات بالكامل عبر Apple. التذكيرات إشعارات محلية. حذف التطبيق يحذف جميع البيانات.
