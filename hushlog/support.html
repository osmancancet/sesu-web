FAQ

I forgot to log a feed or nap. Open History and tap + to add a past entry, or tap any entry to edit its time.

Can my partner see the same log? Hushlog keeps data only on one device for privacy, so there is no account sync. You can export a CSV (Pro) and share it.

How do I track twins? Hushlog Pro lets you add more than one baby in Settings → Babies.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Hushlog → Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before renewal.

I bought Pro but it isn't active. Open Settings in Hushlog and tap Restore Purchases with the same Apple ID.
