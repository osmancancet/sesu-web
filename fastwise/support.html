FAQ

I forgot to start or end a fast. Tap the start time on the timer, or open History → tap a fast to edit its start and end. Use the + button in History to add a past fast.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Fastwise → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I bought Pro but it isn't active. Open Settings in Fastwise and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your data. Pro users can export CSV from Stats.

I'm not getting reminders. iPhone Settings → Notifications → Fastwise → Allow Notifications, and turn on Goal reminders in Fastwise settings.
