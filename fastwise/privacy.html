Fastwise — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Summary: your data stays on your device

Fastwise has no accounts and no server. Your fasts, notes, weight entries and settings are stored only in a database on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to any third party.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information (name, email, location, contacts)
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No ads and no data sharing with ad networks
  • No account or registration required

Purchases

Fastwise Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

Goal and trial reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is used.

Health information

Fastwise is a wellness tool and does not provide medical advice. Fasting and weight data you enter stays on your device and is not shared with Apple Health or any other service.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data. Pro users can export a copy at any time via Stats → Export CSV.

Children

Fastwise is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

سياسة الخصوصية

لا يتطلب Fastwise أي حساب ولا يملك خادمًا. تُحفظ بيانات الصيام والملاحظات والوزن والإعدادات على جهاز iPhone فقط ولا تُرسل إلينا أو إلى أي طرف ثالث. لا نستخدم أدوات تحليل أو تتبع أو إعلانات. تتم عمليات الاشتراك بالكامل عبر Apple ولا نطّلع على بيانات الدفع. التذكيرات إشعارات محلية على جهازك. حذف التطبيق يحذف جميع بياناته.

Gizlilik Politikası

Fastwise hesap gerektirmez ve sunucusu yoktur. Oruç kayıtların, notların, kilo girişlerin ve ayarların yalnızca iPhone'undaki veritabanında saklanır, bize veya üçüncü kişilere asla gönderilmez. Analitik, izleme ya da reklam aracı kullanmayız. Abonelik satın alımları tamamen Apple tarafından yürütülür, ödeme bilgilerini görmeyiz. Hatırlatmalar cihazında planlanan yerel bildirimlerdir. Uygulamayı silmek tüm verileri siler.
