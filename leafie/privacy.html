Leafie — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary: your plants stay on your device

Leafie has no accounts, no server and no ads. Your plants, photos, care history and settings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to us or any third party.

Camera and photos

If you add a plant photo, Leafie uses the camera or photo library only when you choose to. The selected picture is copied into the app's own storage on your device and is never uploaded.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information (name, email, location, contacts)
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No account or registration

Purchases

The current version of Leafie has no in-app purchases. If purchases are offered in the future, they will be processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

Care reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is used.

Deleting your data

Delete a plant to remove its photo and history, or delete the app to remove everything.

لا يتطلب Leafie أي حساب، ولا يملك خادمًا، ولا يعرض إعلانات. تُحفظ نباتاتك وصورها وسجل العناية والإعدادات على جهاز iPhone فقط ولا تُرسل إلى أي جهة. تُستخدم الكاميرا أو الصور فقط عندما تختار إضافة صورة. لا توجد مشتريات داخل التطبيق. التذكيرات إشعارات محلية.
