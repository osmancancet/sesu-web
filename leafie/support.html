FAQ

I watered a plant yesterday but forgot to log it. Open the plant and tap "Watered earlier" to pick the date.

The suggested interval doesn't fit my plant. Intervals are starting suggestions. Edit the plant and change "Water every" — check the soil and adjust to your light and season.

Why don't reminders arrive? iPhone Settings → Notifications → Leafie → Allow Notifications, and turn on the daily reminder in Leafie settings. You'll only get a reminder on days a plant needs care.

How many plants are free? Up to 5 plants with all reminders. Leafie Pro adds unlimited plants and CSV export.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Leafie → Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before renewal.
