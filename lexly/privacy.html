Lexly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Summary: your learning stays on your device

Lexly has no accounts, no server and no ads. Your progress, saved words and settings are stored only on your iPhone and are never sent to us or any third party. All word content is built into the app and works offline.

Pronunciation

Lexly uses the speech voice built into iOS on your device. No audio or text is sent anywhere.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information (name, email, location, contacts)
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs
  • No account or registration

Purchases

The current version of Lexly has no in-app purchases. If purchases are offered in the future, they will be processed entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

Study reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push server is used.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of your progress.

سياسة الخصوصية

لا يتطلب Lexly أي حساب، ولا يملك خادمًا، ولا يعرض إعلانات. يُحفظ تقدمك وكلماتك المحفوظة وإعداداتك على جهاز iPhone فقط ولا تُرسل إلى أي جهة. النطق يستخدم صوت iOS المدمج على جهازك. لا توجد مشتريات داخل التطبيق. التذكيرات إشعارات محلية.
