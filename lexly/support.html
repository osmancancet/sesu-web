FAQ

How does review work? Each word sits in a memory box. Answer right and it moves to the next box and comes back later (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days). Miss it and it returns tomorrow.

Pronunciation doesn't play. Check that your iPhone isn't in Silent mode and the volume is up. Lexly uses the built-in iOS English voice.

What's free? The Everyday pack, 3 new words a day, saving words and all reviews. Lexly Pro adds the Academic, Business and Advanced packs, up to 20 words a day and CSV export.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → Lexly → Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before renewal.
